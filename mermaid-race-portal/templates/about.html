Our Story

The Mermaid Series was founded with a simple mission: make triathlon and running accessible, welcoming, and empowering for women of all ages and ability levels.

From our first race in 2007, we've grown to host events across the country, welcoming thousands of women to their first finish line — and their hundredth.

Our Values

  • Inclusivity — Every level is welcome at the start line
  • Community — Race day is a celebration together
  • Empowerment — We believe every woman can cross the finish line
  • Safety — Well-organized, supported events you can trust
